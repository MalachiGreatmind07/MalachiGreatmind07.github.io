My Story

I grew up in Louisiana and now live in College Park, Maryland. I am a 19 year old Cyber Operations Engineering student at Bowie State University, class of 2028.

In October 2026 I incorporated SMPI, Inc. in Maryland. We build AI research tools for investors, and the goal is simple: help people do their homework before they invest.

Before any of that, I was an athlete. I am a two time AAU All American in track and field, in discus and the 3000m racewalk. Those seasons taught me patience, repetition and how to keep going when progress is slow.

This fall I placed third at the Black Blockchain Summit pitch competition, and I was selected as one of 14 HBCU students for the Howard University x PNC grant. Both meant a lot to me, and both pushed me to build faster.

I also build things on my own time. Brainapedia is a neurology learning site, and Aurum Script is a custom cipher language with its own decoder. My own experience with brain scans made me curious about how the brain works, and that curiosity became Brainapedia.

Projects

Binary Decoder

A browser based tool I built that converts between text and binary and includes Aurum Script, a custom cipher language I designed. It decodes 8 bit and 7 bit binary, continuous bit streams and reversed bit order, and translates text to and from Aurum glyphs using a shifting key. It can also generate translation pairs as training data for AI models. Everything runs in the browser, and output can be copied or downloaded as a text file. Try it below.
