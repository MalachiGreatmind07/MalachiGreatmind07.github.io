Medical Student Study Plan

A routine for learning neurology week by week.

Daily (3 hours or less)

  • 25 new Anki cards and 150 reviews
  • 40 to 60 minutes of active reading
  • 20 to 40 timed, mixed Qbank questions
  • 10 minutes updating an error log
  • 15 to 20 minutes of teach back
  • 7 to 9 hours of sleep, a 30 minute workout, and steady hydration

Weekly

  • Mon: Foundations and 40 questions
  • Tue: Tracts and lesions and 40 questions
  • Wed: Neuropharmacology and 40 questions
  • Thu: Infection, tumor, and trauma and 40 questions
  • Fri: Vascular neurology and 40 questions
  • Sat: 80 to 120 mixed questions
  • Sun: Light review and rest

30 Day Boost

  1. Days 1 to 7: Overview and daily questions
  2. Days 8 to 14: Focus on weak areas
  3. Days 15 to 21: Full question blocks
  4. Days 22 to 27: High yield facts
  5. Days 28 to 30: Two simulated exams and plenty of sleep
